## Full description (4,000 characters max)
Start with nothing. Automate everything. Launch.

You land on Ironfield with empty hands. Mine your first ore by tap, smelt it, craft a Miner, and before long belts are carrying plates past rows of furnaces, inserters are swinging, and generators are humming. Then you build a rocket and leave for somewhere stranger.

8-Bit Industry is a factory automation game made for phones first, in dark pixel art with a chiptune soundtrack. No ads, no energy timers, no paying to skip.

FIVE WORLDS, FIVE PROBLEMS
• Ironfield: belts, inserters, splitters, tunnels and your first rocket
• The Scar: oil, plastic and refined fuel, and the smog it all makes. Keep the air clear or your machines slow down
• The Deep: caves, pipes and pressure. Push too hard and pipes burst. Power your factory by the glow of underground crystals
• The Bloom: living machines that need feeding, and a nuclear reactor that needs cooling
• The Singularity: every resource, every hazard, and one final structure that ends the universe

REAL AUTOMATION
• Belts, splitters, underground tunnels and long inserters
• Machine tiers, modules and belt upgrades with clean per-minute rates
• Recipe Book, production graphs and a power report that find your bottlenecks
• Blueprints, copy and paste, multi-select, undo and redo
• Drag to lay whole belt and pipe lines in one go

KEEP GOING
• Perks after every launch
• The Big Bang: finish the Singularity to earn Stardust for permanent upgrades, then begin a harder universe
• 132 achievements and an optional run timer for speedrunners
• Sandbox mode after your first Big Bang

MADE FOR YOUR PHONE
• Portrait or landscape, phone or tablet
• Plays fully offline
• Colour-blind mode, haptics and a music player in Settings
• Backup codes to move your factory between devices

The factory only runs while the game is open, so it is never waiting on you and never punishing you for putting it down.

The optional Supporter Pack is a one-time thank you that unlocks the Golden Hour theme and Gilded Machines. It changes nothing in the game itself.
