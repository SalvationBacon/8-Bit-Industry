8-Bit Industry: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

In short: 8-Bit Industry collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your factory is saved only on your own device.

What the game stores

Your saves, settings, blueprints, Stardust and best times are stored on your device (in the app's local storage). They never leave your device unless you choose to copy or export a backup code yourself. Uninstalling the app deletes them. On Android, your device's own backup (Google's Android backup) may include the app's data if you have it turned on; that is controlled by your device settings and Google's policies, not by the game.

What the game does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or other device data.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Purchases (Android app)

The optional Supporter Pack is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment under the Google Privacy Policy; the game never sees your payment details. The game only asks Google Play whether the Supporter Pack is owned on your Google account, so it can unlock the cosmetic theme and restore it after a reinstall.

Internet access

The Android app uses the internet only to talk to Google Play for the Supporter Pack. The game itself runs fully offline. The web version at salvationbacon.github.io loads its font from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address under Google's policy.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the contact email on the game's Google Play page, or open an issue at github.com/SalvationBacon/8-Bit-Industry.
