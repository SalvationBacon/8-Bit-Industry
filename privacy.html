8-Bit Industry: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

In short: 8-Bit Industry collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your factory is saved only on your own device.

What the game stores

Your saves, settings, blueprints, Stardust and best times are stored on your device, in your browser's local storage for this site. They never leave your device unless you choose to copy or export a backup code yourself. Clearing your browser's site data, or removing the game from your home screen, can delete them, so keep a backup.

What the game does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or other device data.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Donations

The game is free. If you choose to donate, that happens on Ko-fi, under Ko-fi's own privacy policy. The game never sees your payment details and does not know whether you have donated.

Internet access

The game runs in your browser from salvationbacon.github.io, hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visits under GitHub's privacy policy. It loads its font from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address under Google's policy. Once loaded, the game also works offline.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/SalvationBacon/8-Bit-Industry.
